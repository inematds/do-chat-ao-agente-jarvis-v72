Lesson 8 · 15 min

A real routine and Jarvis up and running

An assistant you don’t use is no use. In this lesson, you turn a weekly task into a standing request, use it five days in a row, and note what to improve. Then you see the steps to keep it running: on your computer, with automatic restarts, and on a rented server, up 24/7.

Start lesson

In this lesson, you will

  1. 1Turn a task into a standing request
  2. 2The weekly improvement cycle
  3. 3Laptop, automatic restart, or server
  4. 4The task list that gets you to step 3

Goal: use a standing request for five days in a row and choose where the assistant will run, from your laptop to a 24/7 server.

01 · Where your Jarvis lives

Four steps, from testing to 24/7. Click each one.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

02 · The routine and improvement

Click each step.

1. Choose the task

Choose one of the three things it should do every week, from the plan in lesson 2.

Pick one thing you’d do anyway. That’s how you build a week of use.

ExampleStart the day with your top three priorities.

2. Make it a standing request

Write the request in AGENTS.md: when you send a short word, it does the task the same way each time.

Scheduling it to run on its own (without you asking) is planned for the next phase. For now, you trigger it.

Standing requestEvery morning, when I send "day," list my top three priorities based on what I told you yesterday.

3. Use it for 5 days

Use it for five days in a row. Write one line each day: what worked and what was missing.

Got the tone wrong? SOUL. Forgot something? Memory. Did something it shouldn’t? AGENTS.

NoteDay 3: listed 5 priorities instead of 3. Update AGENTS.

4. Weekly cycle

Once a week, review your notes and the log, then change just one thing. Run the tests again.

There’s no ready-made assistant. There’s one that gets better with real use.

RuleOne change a week, with tests passing before and after.

Jarvis:

  1. Finish the sales report (it’s Monday).
  2. Reply to the client about the timeline.
  3. Prepare the agenda for Thursday’s meeting.

The three come from what you told it yesterday and from memory.

  • Week 1: the core running on your main channel.
  • Week 2: memory really saving things and one or two tools.
  • Week 3: always on (automatic restart or a server).
  • Week 4: your first round of improvements based on the log and notes.
