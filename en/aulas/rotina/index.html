Lesson 8 · 15 min

A real routine and Jarvis up and running

An assistant you don't use is no good. In this lesson you turn one task from your week into a fixed request, use it five days in a row, and write down what to improve. Then you see the steps for keeping it running: on your computer, with automatic restart, and on a rented server, up 24 hours a day.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Turn a task into a fixed request
  2. 2The weekly improvement cycle
  3. 3Laptop, automatic restart, or server
  4. 4The task list that leads to step 3

Goal: use a fixed request for five days in a row and choose where the assistant will stay running, from your laptop to a 24-hour server.

01 · Where your Jarvis lives

Four steps, from testing to 24 hours. Click each one.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

One at a time: Telegram, then automatic restart, then server. Move to each step only when the previous one is stable.

02 · The routine and the improvement

Tap each step.

1. Choose the task

Pick one of the three things it should do every week, from the lesson 2 design.

Choose one you would do anyway: that way the week of use actually happens.

ExampleStart the day with the three priorities.

2. Turn it into a fixed request

Write the request in AGENTS.md: when you send a short word, it does the task the same way.

Scheduling on its own (sending without you asking) is in the plan for the next phase; for now, you are the one who triggers it.

Fixed requestEvery morning, when I send "dia", list my three priorities based on what I told you yesterday.

3. Use it for 5 days

Use it for five days in a row. Each day, one line: what worked and what was missing.

Wrong tone? SOUL. Forgot something? Memory. Did something it shouldn't have? AGENTS.

NoteDay 3: listed 5 priorities instead of 3. Adjust AGENTS.

4. Weekly cycle

Once a week, look at your notes and the log and change just one thing. Run the tests again.

There is no ready-made assistant: there is one that gets better with real use.

RuleOne change per week, tests green before and after.

  • Week 1: the core running on your main channel.
  • Week 2: memory actually saving, plus one or two tools.
  • Week 3: always on (automatic restart or a server).
